Can gradients be printed?

Yes. These are based on real species, which are darkest on the head and paler toward the tentacle tips. That top-to-bottom fade suits printing well: printed upright, a multi-color printer can switch from a darker to a lighter filament partway down and place each band exactly. Gradient filament also works, though you may see only two or three shades on a piece this small. For the smoothest fade, paint or airbrush it after printing.
